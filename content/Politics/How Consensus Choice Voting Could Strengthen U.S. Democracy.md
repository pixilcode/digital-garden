**Link: [How Consensus Choice Voting Could Strengthen U.S. Democracy](https://www.sir.advancedleadership.harvard.edu/articles/how-consensus-choice-voting-could-strengthen-us-democracy)**

The way voting is currently done, with party primaries coming before general voting, can cause a candidate to get voted in that the majority dislike. **Consensus choice voting** is a solution to that. Primaries are **non-partisan**, and the top three to five candidates are compared **head-to-head** with ranked voting in order to determine what the majority prefers.

_See also: [Better Choices for Democracy](https://www.betterchoices.vote/)_
