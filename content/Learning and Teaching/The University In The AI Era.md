AI is changing how universities operate. Here are some ideas for improving teaching.

_NOTE: this is only a few of the ideas! There are so many awesome ideas in the article, it would probably be more worthwhile to just go read it yourself._

## Gauging understanding

Take-home tests, homework, and projects are no longer as useful in gauging understanding. Instead, **in-person tests** are becoming more useful.

> I think that, from a learning perspective, the butt-in-chair quizzes have been the single most positive change I have made to my classes. I now make quizzes 50% of a student’s grade, and my class grading curve has returned to a reasonable shape."

## AI as a TA

AI can serve as a good TA, especially with the use of a good [`CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` file](https://gist.github.com/1cg/a6c6f2276a1fe5ee172282580a44a7ac).

## Creating visualizations

AI can greatly speed up the process of making visualizations that help the students to understand ideas and processes.

## Everything should be in Markdown

AI can understand Markdown really well, so it makes it easier to use AI when all materials are in Markdown.

## AI vs non-AI Tracks

For competitions, presentations, etc., split groups into AI vs non-AI tracks.
