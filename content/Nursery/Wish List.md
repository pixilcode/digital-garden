## Donations

I like to support causes that I believe in, so one way to give me a gift is to donate to one of those causes!

_If you are looking to give me something physical as a gift, a note/card/letter that says that you donated could work well. And don't worry about telling me how much you donated if you don't want to. Just knowing you donated is a gift to me!_

### Humanitarian and Education

- **A humanitarian group, such as [the Church of Jesus Christ ― Humanitarian Aid](https://give.churchofjesuschrist.org/campaigns/81334/donations/new)** - There are people throughout the world that need help. I can't do much about it where I'm at right now, but donating is one small way that I can show my support.
- **Funding for a school, such as [Brigham Young University Scholarships](https://give.churchofjesuschrist.org/campaigns/81313/donations/new)** - I was blessed to receive scholarships, internships, and research opportunities while I was in college, and I hope some day I can pay it forward!
- **A public library, such as [the Provo City Library](https://provolibrary.gov/financialdonations) or [the Columbus Metropolitan Library](https://www.columbuslibrary.org/foundation/)** - Public libraries are one of the greatest resources a city can have! They offer not just books, but movies, video games, bike repair toolkits, and radon detectors! On top of that, they have some great programs. My favorites have been a 14 week art class and a podcasting class. Seriously, support your local libraries, they deserve it!
- **[Cornell Lab of Ornithology (Birds)](https://give.birds.cornell.edu/page/87895/donate/1)** - I've learned _so much_ about birds from them, which has helped me to appreciate the world around me more! They have free tools such as [Merlin](https://www.merlinbirds.org/), which helps with bird identification; [BirdWise](https://app.academy.allaboutbirds.org/dashboard), which has great quizzes about bird identification; and [Blird](https://app.academy.allaboutbirds.org/games/blird), a fun game where you try and guess the bird by clicking on blurred tiles to unblur them. Because of these games and tools, I now know the difference between a house finch and a hermit thrush.
- **[FIRST](https://www.firstinspires.org/donate)** - When I was in high school, I did [_FIRST LEGO League_](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/fll/), [_FIRST Tech Challenge_](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/ftc/), and [_FIRST Robotics Competition_](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/frc/). These programs had a big impact on me, and they helped me decide to pursue computer science further.

### Free/Open Source Resources

- **[Wikipedia](https://donate.wikimedia.org/w/index.php)** - An online encyclopedia with any information you could every dream of. Need a list of lists of lists? [They've got it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_lists_of_lists). Wondering how you can win at Scrabble? Check out their [list of English words containing Q not followed by U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_English_words_containing_Q_not_followed_by_U). Looking for a fun party game? Try [PowerPoint karaoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PowerPoint_karaoke).
- **[LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/donate/)** - An open source office suite, with tools for writing documents, editing spreadsheets, and the like. Why pay for Office 365 when you've got LibreOffice to meet all your needs?
- **[AnkiDroid](https://opencollective.com/ankidroid)** - A mobile application for [Anki](https://ankiweb.net/). If I haven't evangelized to you about Anki and spaced repetition, ask me sometime. Just make sure you have the rest of your day free. 🙃
- **[Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/id/support-us/donate/)** - A scalable vector graphics (SVG) editor. I use this all the time for diagrams, simple backgrounds, and other random uses.
- **[Signal](https://signal.org/donate/)** - A secure messaging app with end-to-end encryption by default. I appreciate being able to message people without worrying that my messages are being intercepted by some malicious actor.
- **[VLC](https://www.videolan.org/contribute.html)** - A free media player for videos and music. I've recently been trying to own my music, so this is what I use to play the MP3 files that I have.
- **[GrapheneOS](https://grapheneos.org/donate)** - An operating system for Android phones that enables privacy and security by default. I have a lot more control over what data can be used, so I don't feel like my data is being constantly harvested.
- **[GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/donating/)** - An image editor, similar to Photoshop. I use it for my various image editing needs, and it works pretty well!

### Advocacy Groups

- **[Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://supporters.eff.org/donate/join-eff-4)** - Technology is an amazing thing that enables us to do a lot more things! However, there are also opportunities for companies and governments to abuse the technology in order to make a bit of money or silence views they don't agree with. The Electronic Frontier Foundation is working to increase online privacy for all by advocating in courts and legislative groups, as well as by providing tools like [Privacy Badger](https://www.eff.org/pages/privacy-badger) and [Certbot](https://www.eff.org/pages/certbot) for improving the internet.
- **[The League of American Bicyclists](https://give.bikeleague.org/page/104871/donate/1)** - I _love_ biking. It's how I get exercise after sitting at a computer all day. And it's a lot faster than walking or running. And it's more versatile and maneuverable than a car. And it's a lot cheaper than buying a car and paying for gas. However, in spite of all of these _amazing_ benefits, some places still don't have good infrastructure for biking! It's quite tragic... Well, the League of American Bicyclists is working to improve on that!
