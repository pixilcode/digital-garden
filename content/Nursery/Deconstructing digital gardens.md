**Link: [Deconstructing digital gardens](https://vivqu.com/blog/2020/10/18/digital-gardens/)**

Digital gardens are a newer evolution of writing where people have more "informal" notes that they connect. They are intended to be less formal and easily digestible. However, they often can cause a reader to diverge into multiple paths that are hard to follow.

There are also ways to improve blog posts to be less formal and perfectionistic without turning it into a chaotic set of "mini essays".
