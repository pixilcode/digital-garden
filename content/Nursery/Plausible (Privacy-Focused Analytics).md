**Plausible** is an analytics software that is intended to be lightweight and focused on privacy. I tried it on my current web server, but I've only got 1GB of RAM, and it requires at least 2GB. At some point in the future, I'd like to give it a shot. It would be kinda fun to see how many people are actually reading the stuff on my website! And I wouldn't have to feel bad about identifying or tracking anyone that visits.

- [Github repository](https://github.com/plausible/analytics)
- [Community edition installation guide](https://github.com/plausible/community-edition/)
- [Setting up with a reverse proxy like nginx](https://github.com/plausible/community-edition/wiki/reverse-proxy)
