**Link: [Evaporative Cooling of Group Beliefs](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/ZQG9cwKbct2LtmL3p/evaporative-cooling-of-group-beliefs)**

When we immediately eject any voice of dissension from a community, the opinions of that community get more extreme, since the community becomes an echo chamber. Instead, it's important to keep people around who have a slight disagreement with the "community stance" so that they can keep the extremity of the opinion in check.
